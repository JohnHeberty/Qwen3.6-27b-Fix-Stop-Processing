Learning All Possible Policies with
Entropy Methods
Deep reinforcement learning (RL) is a standard tool due to its ability to process and
approximate complex observations, which result in elaborate behaviors. However,
many deep RL methods optimize for a deterministic policy, since if you had full
observability, there is only one best policy. But it is often desirable to learn a stochas‐
tic policy or probabilistic behaviors to improve robustness and deal with stochastic
environments.
