Algorithm performance measures
I would suggest that in most industrial problems you have a single or a small number
of problems to target and therefore a small number of environments. However, if you
are more involved in pure research or you are trying to adapt your implementation to
generalize over different problems, then you should consider more abstract perfor‐
mance measures that explain how well it performs at RL-specific challenges.
One of the most useful solutions so far is the behavior suite by Osband et al. They
describe seven fundamental RL challenges: credit assignment, basic (a competency
test, like bandit problems), scalability, noise, memory, generalization, and explora‐
tion. An agent is tested against a handpicked set of environments that correspond to
the challenge. The agent then receives a final aggregated score for each challenge that
can be represented in a radar plot, like in Figure 10-8.21
