All of the RL algorithms presented up to this point have performed a least-squares-
like optimization, where estimates of a value approximation are compared to an
observed result; if you observe a surprising result, then you update the estimate
accordingly. In an off-policy setting, the target policy never has the opportunity to
test its estimate, so how can you perform optimization?
