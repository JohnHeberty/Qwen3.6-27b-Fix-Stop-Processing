Figure 2-5. A graph representing the transition probabilities of the simple inventory
problem.
The graph has two types of nodes (called vertices): one for the state, represented by a
circle with the name of that state inside, and another for the action, represented by a
solid circle. These connect through links (called edges), labeled with the probability
of following that link after the action (the transition probability) and the reward
obtained after following that link.
