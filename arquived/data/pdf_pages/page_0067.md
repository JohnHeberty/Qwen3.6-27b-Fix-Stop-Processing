You have just derived your first set of policies. And I haven’t yet discussed how to
learn an optimal policy—that will take the rest of the book. But this is an important
result to take in; an optimal policy is dependent on all components of an MDP.
Changing the environment to include more stock, or allowing people to buy more
than one item at a time, affects the best restocking strategy. Altering the reward func‐
tion by introducing a penalty for ordering—a charge for delivery, for example—also
alters the best strategy. In these cases it might be better to wait until you have zero
stock then order a full 2 units worth of product. The reward function defines what
problem you want solved, not how to achieve it.
